3. Definitions
To help you understand this Policy, the following terms are used:
* Personal Data / Personal Information: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (a "Data Principal" under the DPDP Act, or "Data Subject" under the GDPR).
* Processing: Any operation performed on personal data, such as collection, recording, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
* Data Fiduciary / Data Controller: The entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data. For the purposes of this Policy, MahiiVerse is the Data Fiduciary/Controller.

* Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw that consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

* Right to grievance redressal: Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to a readily available means to raise grievances regarding the processing of your personal data.
* Right to nominate: Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
