FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

COMMON QUESTIONS
ANSWERED.

Who can participate in MahiiVerse internship programs?

Our programs are designed primarily for students, college undergraduates, post-graduates, and aspiring professionals looking to gain practical, hands-on exposure in software, hardware, AI, engineering, and digital growth domains.

How long are the internship programs?

Program duration varies depending on the domain, curriculum structure, and student or institutional requirements. Typical formats range from fast-paced 4-week tracks to comprehensive 8-to-12-week semester-aligned internships.

Can colleges customize the program?

Yes. Programs can be fully customized based on specific engineering or degree departments, student foundations, duration, academic calendars, and university accreditation objectives.

Do students receive certificates?

Yes. Students who successfully complete the coursework and capstone project submissions receive an official verified certificate of completion from MahiiVerse.

Are the programs project-based?

Absolutely. Every track is built around application-first learning. Students work on guided exercises and build production-style projects or simulations that can be showcased on GitHub and portfolios.

Does MahiiVerse provide placement opportunities?

Eligible participants gain access to our placement pool, hiring partners, portfolio review workshops, and career orientation sessions to accelerate their job search.

Can MahiiVerse conduct programs directly for our college?

Yes. We actively collaborate with colleges and universities across India for on-campus orientations, technical workshops, institutional MoUs, and faculty/student training programs.

How can our institution collaborate with MahiiVerse?

Simply reach out through our contact form below or email us at enquiry.mahiiverse@gmail.com. Our team will schedule a discovery call to recommend a suitable program structure.
