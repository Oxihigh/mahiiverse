Common Questions

FREQUENTLY
ASKED.

How does the discovery process work?

Every engagement starts with a structured discovery phase — typically 1–2 weeks. We interview stakeholders, audit existing systems, and document findings before proposing any solution. This is non-negotiable and is the foundation of everything we deliver.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Absolutely. We have specific frameworks for startup engagements — fast MVPs, lean validation builds, and scalable architectures that grow with your product. We're experienced in the startup context and adapt accordingly.

What does an AI agent engagement look like?

We start by mapping the specific workflow you want to automate, define the agent's scope and success criteria, then build and test iteratively — with UAT done in your real environment before full deployment.

Can you handle both tech and marketing for us?

Yes — and we'd encourage it. Having one partner that understands both your product and your brand creates strategic coherence that separate agencies rarely achieve. Many of our best outcomes have come from integrated engagements.

Do you travel for on-site work?

Yes. We operate pan-India and will travel to your location if required for workshops, and key milestones. We combine in-person and remote working based on what makes sense for your project.
